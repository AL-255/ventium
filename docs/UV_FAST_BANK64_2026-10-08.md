### Read-only counter audit, 15:03 UTC

The frozen `ventium_top.sv` increments `retire_n` by one or two and exports it
as `retire_count`. However, `ven_ao486_shim.sv` only connects that output to the
otherwise unused `v_retire_count`; its board debug bus carries the primary
retirement pulse, not the total counter or V pulse. `ven_soc_dbg.sv` increments
`perf_retired` by one on that primary pulse, and the architectural selector map
has no retirement-counter entry. Thus this ABI cannot supply total U+V IPC via
the existing mapped debug interface. Keep the primary-counter label; neither
an unmapped read nor a paired-ring sample supplies the missing total. No RTL,
debug ABI or live build input was changed for this audit.
